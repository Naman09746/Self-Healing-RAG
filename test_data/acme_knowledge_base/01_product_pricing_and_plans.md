# AcmeCloud — Product Pricing, Plans & Trial Policy

## 1. Subscription Plans Overview
AcmeCloud offers three core tiers to accommodate teams of any scale:

* **Starter Plan**: Free tier for individual developers. Includes 1 team member and basic community support.
* **Professional Plan**: Priced at **$49 per month** (billed monthly or annually). Supports **up to 25 team members** per workspace. Includes priority chat and email customer support with a target response time within 1 business day.
* **Enterprise Plan**: Custom enterprise pricing for large organizations. Supports **unlimited team members**, custom role-based access control, dedicated account manager, and enterprise support response targets within 4 business hours.

## 2. 14-Day Free Trial Policy
* All new organizations are eligible for a **14-day free trial** of the Professional Plan.
* **No credit card is required** to start the 14-day trial.
* The trial automatically expires at the conclusion of 14 days unless the organization explicitly upgrades to a paid plan.
* Workspaces that do not upgrade revert to read-only status after trial expiration.
