# AcmeCloud — Customer Support Tiers & Service Level Agreement (SLA)

## 1. Customer Support Channels & Response Times
Customer support availability varies according to the active subscription plan:

* **Starter Support**: Asynchronous community forums and documentation access.
* **Professional Support**: Includes **priority chat and email support**. The target initial response time is **within 1 business day** (Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM EST).
* **Enterprise Support**: Includes **email, priority chat, and a dedicated account manager / technical support engineer**. Enterprise inquiries have an initial response time target of **within 4 business hours** with 24/7 emergency incident coverage.

## 2. Service Level Agreement (SLA) Scope
* Enterprise contracts include a negotiated **99.99% monthly uptime guarantee** backed by financial service credits.
* The public documentation does not specify a 99.99% uptime SLA for Professional plan customers.
